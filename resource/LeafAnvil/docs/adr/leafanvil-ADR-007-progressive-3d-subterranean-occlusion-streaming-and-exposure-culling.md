## Context

Under `leafanvil-ADR-003`, `leafanvil-ADR-005`, and `leafanvil-ADR-006`, `LeafAnvil` and the standalone `MIT`-licensed wire carrier `:resource:LeafAnvil:leafanvil-net` stream `.lca` chunk columns over `LCA_NET_V1` (`PROTOCOL_VERSION = 1` in `LcaWireStreamProtocol` and `LcaRemoteStreamCodec`), achieving `65%–78%` initial and `95%+` revisit bandwidth reductions relative to vanilla `ClientboundLevelChunkWithLightPacket`. Because `:resource:LeafAnvil:leafanvil-net` has not yet been publicly released, `PROTOCOL_VERSION = 1` carries zero legacy client compatibility debt and can incorporate progressive 3D spatial streaming natively from v1.0.

Inspecting `TrackedChunkColumn#toWireFullChunkEntry` (`LcaInMemoryChunkCoordinator`), `LcaWireStreamProtocol`, `PaperLeafAnvilPlugin`, `PaperLcaNettyChannelHandler`, and `FabricLeafAnvilClientMod` / `NeoForgeLeafAnvilClientMod` exposes four remaining bandwidth, client-performance, Paper-CPU, and anti-xray gaps:

1. **Unconditional Full-Column Subterranean Transmission (`Y = -64..319`):**
   - `TrackedChunkColumn#toWireFullChunkEntry` currently serializes all `24` Overworld vertical sections on every initial `EntryFullChunk` send—even when a player is flying with Elytra, boating across an ocean, or random-teleporting via `LeafRTP` on the surface (`Y >= 62`) and cannot visually see or physically reach `7–12` enclosed subterranean cave, ravine, mineshaft, Trial Chamber, Ancient City, or ore-bearing sections (`150–300 B` each) per column.
   - On the client, hydrating unreachable underground sections inflates `ClientChunkSlabCache` (`32 MiB` default budget), attaches unseen subterranean `BlockEntity` tickers, and causes `Sodium` / `Embeddium` `SectionCompiler` workers to mesh thousands of occluded cave triangles beneath the surface.
2. **Paper `ChunkPacketBlockControllerAntiXray` Region-Thread Tax & Current `LCA_NET_V1` Anti-Xray Bypass:**
   - Paper's built-in `anti-xray` (`engine-mode: 1` or `2` in `paper-world.yml`) mutates outgoing `ClientboundLevelChunkPacketData` inline on the server/Folia region thread and injects high-entropy fake-ore noise in `engine-mode: 2` that inflates `Zlib` chunk packets by `2x–3x`.
   - Worse, in `PaperLcaNettyChannelHandler#write`, `LeafAnvil` currently releases Paper's obfuscated `ClientboundLevelChunkWithLightPacket` (`ReferenceCountUtil.release(msg)`) and encodes `TrackedChunkColumn#toWireFullChunkEntry` directly from un-sanitized `.lca` slices—meaning negotiated `LCA_NET_V1` clients bypass Paper's `anti-xray` controller completely, while mixed servers still pay Paper's region-thread anti-xray CPU cost on discarded packets.
3. **First-RTT `PLAY`-Stage Handshake Leak & ClientChunkCache Installation Gap:**
   - `FabricLeafAnvilClientMod` and `NeoForgeLeafAnvilClientMod` currently transmit `leafanvil:chunk_stream_handshake` after entering `PLAY`, and `PaperLeafAnvilPlugin#onPluginMessageReceived` injects `PaperLcaNettyChannelHandler` only upon receipt. During that initial `1`-RTT window, `PlayerChunkSender` already dispatches the initial spawn ring as unmasked vanilla `ClientboundLevelChunkWithLightPacket`s.
   - Furthermore, when `PaperLcaNettyChannelHandler` suppresses `ClientboundLevelChunkWithLightPacket` for subsequent chunks, `FabricLeafAnvilClientMod#commitHydratedChunkOnClientMainThread` and `NeoForgeLeafAnvilClientMod#commitHydratedChunkOnClientMainThread` currently guard on `level.getChunkSource().hasChunk(chunkX, chunkZ)` without allocating missing `LevelChunk` shells or delivering per-section `BlockEntity` update tags inside `EntryFullChunk` / `EntryPatchDelta`.
4. **Adoption Incentives ("Optimization First, Anti-Xray as a Free Consequence"):**
   - Players and modpack authors eagerly adopt `MIT` client optimization mods (`Sodium`, `Lithium`, `Bobby`, `Distant Horizons`) that speed up chunk loading, raise client FPS, and expand local chunk caching, whereas mods framed solely as mandatory server anti-cheat face adoption friction.
   - Designing progressive 3D subterranean occlusion primarily as a **bandwidth, server-CPU, and client-FPS streaming optimization**—with optional `network.require-lca-client: true` fail-closed enforcement for modpacks and competitive survival shards—delivers immediate value on open mixed servers (`require-lca-client: false` default) while turning cave/stash/ore anti-xray into a zero-bloat structural side effect when required.

## Decision

Extend `LCA_NET_V1` (`PROTOCOL_VERSION = 1`) across `:anvil-api` and `:resource:LeafAnvil` with a **4-layer Progressive 3D Subterranean Occlusion Streaming & Zero-Bloat Exposure Culling architecture**:

```
Disk / LeafGen K3 Cold .lca ChunkFrame (RawChunkFrameSnapshot)
  + SparseDirtyCellMap (leafanvil-ADR-006)
        |
        +--> [Once per (column, lastUpdateTick), player-independent]
        |      1. Extract transmissiveOctantMask64[sectionCount] (192 B) & lithicHostRuntimeStateId[sectionCount]
        |      2. Optional Layer 2: Build wireSanitizedBaseFrame (strip unexposed xraySensitive minority runs;
        |         preserve 100% ODCT content-addressed slot sharing across all players)
        |
        +--> [Per player or 3D MTCF spatial-stratum cell, off-tick]
               3. Layer 1: Surface Envelope | 3x3x3 Velocity/F5 Halo | Exact Orthant-Monotone Sightline
                  & Turn-Budgeted 3D Bitwise BFS -> SectionBitMask (uint32 fast path / long[] for >32 sections)
               4. Masked sections emit 2 B inline Uniform (or 4-5 B Mode-00 Uniform if runtimeId > 8,191)
                  + 0 withheld section BlockEntities/Entities; reachable upgrades stream via EntryPatchDelta
```

### 1. Layer 1 — `.lca`-Native 64-Bit Optical Octant Transmittance & Monotone-Sightline 3D Reachability

#### 1.1 Compressed-Domain `transmissiveOctantMask64` Extraction (`:anvil-api`, Optical Non-Occlusion Over Walkability)
Using physical walkability alone would falsely mask sealed underground rooms, aquaria, or vaults viewed through `glass`, `tinted_glass`, `iron_bars`, `ice`, `slabs`, `stairs`, or `trapdoors`. Layer 1 therefore defines octant connectivity by **optical transmittance (`!isFullOpaqueCube(runtimeStateId)`, i.e. `!BlockState#canOcclude()`)**, which strictly subsumes all walkable/swimmable tunnels and transparent/partial-block sightlines while treating `100%` of solid subterranean rock (`stone`, `deepslate`, `tuff`, `granite`, `diorite`, `andesite`, `netherrack`, `blackstone`, `basalt`, `obsidian`, `bedrock`, and full-cube `ores`) as opaque barriers.

Under `anvil-api-ADR-003` Sections `4.1` and `5`, each `TrackedChunkColumn` derives and caches a `long[sectionCount]` `transmissiveOctantMask64` array (`192 B` for `24` sections, indexed in `01:H_3` $4 \times 4 \times 4$ octant order $k \in [0, 63]$) plus `int[sectionCount]` `lithicHostRuntimeStateId` directly from `RawChunkFrameSnapshot` and `LcaSectionBacking`'s `SparseDirtyCellMap` without inflating `int[4096]` voxel arrays (`REQ-RTP-S-005`):
1. **Inline Uniform Directory Word (`sectionOffsets[i]`):**
   - `entry16 == 0x0000` (unallocated sky `air`): `transmissiveOctantMask64[i] = -1L` (`64` transmissive octants).
   - `(entry16 & 0x8000) != 0`: resolves single state `s0` in $O(1)$; sets `-1L` if `!isFullOpaqueCube(s0)`, else `0L` (`66.4%` of natural sections finish in `< 5 ns`).
2. **Micro-Palette Header Short-Circuit (`bit 15 == 0`):**
   - Scans only the `VarInt[|P|]` section palette header (`palette[0 .. |P|-1]`).
   - If `palette[0]` is a full opaque cube and **every** minority entry $p \in [1, |P|-1]$ also satisfies `isFullOpaqueCube(palette[p])` (e.g., solid `stone` / `deepslate` / `netherrack` containing only `diamond_ore`, `ancient_debris`, `tuff`, `granite`, or `gravel`), `transmissiveOctantMask64[i] = 0L` immediately **without reading a single Delta-Roaring container byte**.
3. **Selective Transmissive-Container Accumulation:**
   - When `palette[0]` is a full opaque cube and only a subset $P_{\text{trans}} \subset \{1 \dots |P|-1\}$ is optically transmissive, decodes **only** the containers $p \in P_{\text{trans}}$ (bounded strictly within `octreeOccupancyMask64`) and sets bit $k$ whenever a decoded voxel lands in $4 \times 4 \times 4$ octant $k$.
   - When `palette[0]` itself is optically transmissive (e.g., hollow cavern interior where `cave_air`, `water`, or `lava` is majority), starts from `-1L` and clears octant bit $k$ only if all $64$ voxels in octant $k$ belong to full-opaque-cube minority containers.
   - Overlays any uncompacted entries in `SparseDirtyCellMap` (`O(D)` sparse probes, incrementing a per-column `topologyEpoch` only when an octant transitions between `0` transmissive voxels and `>= 1` transmissive voxels).

#### 1.2 Dominant Opaque Lithic Host State & `> 8,191` Runtime-ID Overflow Guard
Naively masking an unreachable subterranean section to `palette[0]` has two subtle traps that Layer 1 explicitly prevents:
1. **Hollow-Cavern `palette[0] == cave_air / water / lava` Leak Prevention:**
   - Inside large sealed caverns, Ancient Cities, aquifers, or subterranean lava lakes, frequency-sorted `palette[0]` is `cave_air`, `water`, or `lava` rather than rock. Emitting `Uniform(cave_air)` for a sealed section would expose the cavern's exact 3D bounding box to CaveFinder mods and force `Sodium` to mesh `1,536` internal cavity faces against neighboring rock sections.
   - For every mask-eligible section $i$, the server resolves **`lithicHostRuntimeStateId[i]`** as the highest-frequency **opaque full-cube terrain state** in `palette[0 .. |P|-1]` (`stone`, `deepslate`, `netherrack`, `blackstone`, `tuff`, `end_stone`), falling back to the dimension/elevation stratum host (`minecraft:deepslate[axis=y]` for $Y < 0$ and `minecraft:stone` for $Y \ge 0$ in Overworld; `minecraft:netherrack` in Nether) when a section is `100%` `cave_air`/fluid (`|P| == 1`).
2. **13-Bit `encodeUniformDirectoryWord` Overflow Fallback (`runtimeStateId > 8,191`):**
   - `LcaWireStreamProtocol#encodeUniformDirectoryWord` packs `majorityStateId & 0x1FFF` (`0..8,191`). On the `LCA_NET_V1` wire, IDs are global network `Block.BLOCK_STATE_REGISTRY` IDs where `minecraft:stone` is `<= 8,191` (`2 B`), whereas `minecraft:deepslate[axis=y]` (`~24,500+` in MC `1.21.x`/`26.x`) and modded dimension stones exceed `8,191`.
   - Proposed helper `SectionWireSlice#maskedUniform(int sectionIndex, int lithicHostRuntimeStateId, int skyLight2)` emits the `2-byte` inline directory word when `lithicHostRuntimeStateId <= 0x1FFF` and automatically emits `anvil-api-ADR-003` Section `4.1`'s **`4–5 byte` `blockEncodingMode = 00` (`Uniform`, `skyLightMode = 00`, `blockLightMode = 00`, `paletteLen = 1`, `VarInt lithicHostRuntimeStateId`) `SectionFrame`** when `lithicHostRuntimeStateId > 0x1FFF`—which `LcaOrderlessDictTable` (`ODCT`) immediately compresses to a `2-byte` `refWord16` slot across subsequent sections without 13-bit ID truncation.

#### 1.3 Dimension-General Surface Envelope & Monotone-Sightline + Turn-Budgeted Octant BFS (`LcaOctantOcclusionEngine`)
Because `SkyLight` is `0` everywhere in `minecraft:the_nether` and `minecraft:the_end`—and because straight sightlines inside giant Overworld cheese caverns or vertical `1x1` drop shafts can exceed short walking radii—proposed `LcaOctantOcclusionEngine` (in `net.leaf.anvil.common.net`) evaluates section visibility per dimension archetype without false clipping walls:

1. **Dimension Policy Classification & Tall-Datapack Bitset Safety (`sectionCount > 32`):**
   - **Variable-Height `SectionBitMask` (`1..254` Sections):** Standard `16..32`-section worlds (`Y = -64..319`, `24` sections) pack completion masks into a single `int32` fast path (`completedSectionMask32`), while custom datapack dimensions with `sectionCount > 32` (up to MC's `254`-section / `4,064`-block ceiling) use `long[ceil(sectionCount / 64)]` words (`VarInt wordCount` on wire), eliminating Java `1 << sectionIndex` 32-bit shift-wrap bugs.
   - **Open-Void Skylightless (`!hasSkyLight && !hasCeiling`, e.g. `minecraft:the_end`):** Unbounded void sightlines with `> 85%` already-`2 B` `0x0000` air sections; Layer 1 section masking defaults to `DISABLED` (`completedSections = allSections`), while optional Layer 2 buried-block culling remains available.
   - **Skylit Surface (`hasSkyLight && !hasCeiling`, e.g. `minecraft:overworld`):** Computes `surfaceEnvelopeMask` per column as all sections with `skyLightMode != 00` (or inline `skyLight2 != 0`) **unioned with** all sections down to `(minOceanFloorY >> 4) - surfaceCrustSections` (`default = 1` section below the column's minimum `Heightmap.Types.OCEAN_FLOOR` $Y$). Including `OCEAN_FLOOR` is load-bearing for deep oceans (`Y = 30..62`): water attenuates `SkyLight` by `1` per block (`SkyLight -> 0` below `Y = 47`), so relying on `SkyLight > 0` alone would falsely mask deep-ocean water sections (`Y = 32..46`) to solid rock beneath boats and swimmers, while `Heightmap.primeHeightmaps` (`CAP_CLIENT_HEIGHTMAPS`) retains `100%` exact surface and ocean-floor stops on the client.
   - **Enclosed Ceiling (`hasCeiling`, e.g. `minecraft:the_nether` and custom cave dimensions):** `surfaceEnvelopeMask = 0`; visibility is driven entirely by the player safety halo plus 3D octant reachability below, masking `100%` of solid `netherrack` sections (`transmissiveOctantMask64 == 0L`, where buried `ancient_debris` at `Y = 8..22` and hidden Nether bases reside) and disconnected pockets while keeping open Nether lava caverns visible to full `viewDistance`.
2. **Unconditional Velocity-Swept & `F5`-Camera `3x3x3`-Section Safety + Biome-Blend Halo:**
   - All sections within a `3x3x3`-section (`48x48x48`-block) cube centered on the player's current section **and** swept along `position + velocity * velocityLeadTicks` (`default = 8` ticks, covering terminal-velocity free-fall `~31` blocks, third-person `F5` `4.0`-block camera offset, and Elytra dive bursts) are unconditionally included in `completedSections`.
   - **3D Biome Interpolation Proof:** Because Minecraft's `BiomeManager#getNoiseBiomeAtQuart` and `Sodium`'s biome blend (`<= 15` blocks) sample at most `1` section across any border from the camera, keeping the `3x3x3`-section halo unmasked guarantees `100%` exact `Lush Caves`, `Deep Dark`, water-tint, fog, and ambient audio transitions with zero biome-fallback artifacts.
3. **Exact Orthant-Monotone Sightline Wave + Winding-Path Prefetch Horizon (`< 40 us` off-tick):**
   - **Exact Ray Monotonicity Property:** Along any continuous straight visual ray $\mathbf{r}(t) = \mathbf{o} + t\mathbf{d}$ ($t \ge 0$) passing through non-occluding voxels, the $4 \times 4 \times 4$ octant coordinates $\lfloor \mathbf{r}(t) / 4 \rfloor$ are strictly monotone (non-decreasing when $d_{\text{axis}} \ge 0$, non-increasing when $d_{\text{axis}} \le 0$) in one of the `8` sign orthants $(\pm 1, \pm 1, \pm 1)$ with **zero** reverse-axis steps. Seeding from the `3x3x3`-octant (`12x12x12`-block) camera neighborhood around the player's eyes covers eye-height crouching/swimming, mounts, and `F5` third-person camera offsets.
   - **Wave A (Orthant-Monotone Sightline Wave, bounded by `playerViewDistance`):** Propagates 6-connected (plus corner-graze diagonal when shared face octants are transmissive) bitwise shifts over `transmissiveOctantMask64` within each of the `8` sign orthants. Straight `1x1` vertical drop shafts, underground glass-window vaults, open surface ravine mouths, `120`-block Overworld Lush/Cheese caverns, and `16`-chunk Nether lava oceans remain `100%` unmasked along every physically unoccluded visual ray (`falseOccludedSightlineCount == 0`).
   - **Wave B (Non-Monotone Winding-Tunnel Prefetch Wave, bounded by `windingPrefetchOctants`, `default = 12` octants = `48` blocks, hysteresis `D_send = 12` / `D_retain = 16`):** Propagates around cave bends and multi-turn shafts where geometric line-of-sight is physically blocked by rock walls, streaming newly reachable sections via `EntryPatchDelta` well ahead of player sprinting/mining speed while withholding deeper winding networks and `100%` of topologically disconnected (`d_3D = infinity`) caves, structures, stashes, and `0L`-transmissive solid ore sections.
4. **Pre-Teleport Destination Halo Flush (`REQ-RTP-S-001`) & Permission-Gated `SPECTATOR` Policy:**
   - On `LeafRTP` `TeleportPipelineTask` completion, Ender Pearl stasis triggers, `/tp`, portals, or respawn anchors targeting an already-client-cached chunk, `LcaPlayerOcclusionTracker` evaluates `completedSections` around `(destX, destY, destZ)` and flushes the destination `EntryPatchDelta` **prior to** `ClientboundPlayerPositionPacket` on the channel so the client never collides with placeholder rock.
   - To prevent survival alt-accounts using spectator-camera triggers from scouting buried bases while preserving staff moderation, `occlusion.spectator-policy` supports `HALO_ONLY | FULL_IF_PERMITTED | FULL` (`default = FULL_IF_PERMITTED`, granting full-column unmasking only when `player` holds `leafanvil.bypass.occlusion` / OP, and moving a local `3x3x3` halo otherwise).

### 2. Layer 2 — Player-Independent Zero-Bloat Exposure Culling (`wireSanitizedBaseFrame`)

Once a cave section $i$ is within reachability ($i \in \mathtt{completedSections}$) and transmitted, ores or stash blocks buried `1–3` blocks behind that cave's rock walls would still be readable by client xray mods unless sanitized. Unlike Paper `engine-mode: 2` (which injects random fake-ore noise that destroys `Zlib`/`LCD2` compression), `.lca` sanitizes buried blocks **by strict byte removal** once per `(TrackedChunkColumn, lastUpdateTick)`:

1. **Lock-Free Folia-Safe 6-Neighbor Exposure Predicate & Dekker-Safe Seam Resolution:**
   - A voxel $v \in [0, 4095]$ in section $i$ is **exposed** iff at least one of its `6` face-adjacent neighbors (`±X`, `±Y`, `±Z`) satisfies `!isFullOpaqueCube(neighborState)` (air, water, lava, glass, slabs, stairs, torches, sculk veins, etc.).
   - **Tear-Free CoW `FaceBitmap256` Across Folia Region Boundaries (`F-001`):** Adjacent chunks `(cx, cz)` and `(cx ± 1, cz)` may be mutated concurrently by distinct Folia region threads; acquiring cross-column locks risks AB-BA deadlocks, while mutating a `long[4]` face bitmap in place risks torn reads. Each tracked section therefore publishes its `6` `16x16` border-transmittance bitmaps as immutable Copy-on-Write `FaceBitmap256(long w0, long w1, long w2, long w3, long faceEpoch)` records via a `volatile` reference (`VarHandle` release/acquire fences matching `anvil-api-ADR-003` `CBIT T8`), reading already-resident neighbor `TrackedChunkColumn` snapshots lock-free **without ever triggering synchronous neighbor chunk loads (`REQ-RTP-S-005`)**.
   - **Canonical-Endpoint `activeChunks#compute` Seam Handshake (`0` Dekker Races on Concurrent Load):** Initial `FaceBitmap256` snapshots are fully populated *prior* to atomic publication via `ConcurrentHashMap#compute` into `LcaInMemoryChunkCoordinator.activeChunks` (establishing a JMM happens-before write barrier before any neighbor probe). Each shared horizontal seam (`(cx, cz) +X <-> (cx + 1, cz) -X` and `(cx, cz) +Z <-> (cx, cz + 1) -Z`) is arbitrated on a single canonical `seamResolvedMask` `CAS` owned by the lexicographically smaller `(cx, cz)` column so concurrent `bindColdChunkFrame` workers never double-emit or miss `4-byte` `dirtyOverlay` seam reveals.
2. **Dual-Case `palette[0]` Soundness & `CBIT T6b` Octree Syndrome Preservation in `LcaSectionCodec` (`:anvil-api`):**
   - **Case 2A (`palette[0]` is opaque lithic terrain — `> 92%` of cave sections):** `LcaSectionCodec` filters each `xraySensitive` minority container $p \in [1, |P|-1]$ (`#minecraft:coal_ores`, `#minecraft:iron_ores`, `#minecraft:diamond_ores`, `#minecraft:gold_ores`, `#minecraft:redstone_ores`, `#minecraft:lapis_ores`, `#minecraft:emerald_ores`, `#minecraft:copper_ores`, `ancient_debris`, `raw_*_block`, configurable stash/spawner blocks) to retain **only exposed voxel indices**. Because `.lca` implicitly fills every omitted voxel index with `palette[0]` (`stone` / `deepslate` / `netherrack`), unexposed ores vanish into host rock at **negative wire byte cost** (`wireSanitizedBaseFrame.length <= rawBaseFrame.length`). Containers whose exposed count drops to `0` are pruned from the wire section's `VarInt` micro-palette altogether.
   - **`CBIT T6b` Rank-Compacted Cursor Re-Indexing (`decodedOctreeMask64 == sanitizedOctreeMask64`):** Under `anvil-api-ADR-003` Sections `5.2` and `7.1`, `blockEncodingMode == 01` rank-compacts container delta cursors over the set bits of `octreeOccupancyMask64` and verifies `decodedOctreeMask64 == octreeOccupancyMask64` (`CBIT T6b`) on decode. Whenever Case 2A removes the last minority voxel from an octant $k$, `LcaSectionCodec` clears bit $k$ in `sanitizedOctreeMask64` (collapsing the entire section to a `2 B` / `4–5 B` uniform `lithicHost` slice when `sanitizedOctreeMask64 == 0L`) and re-indexes surviving container gaps over `sanitizedOctreeMask64` so client and Rust `lca-format` (`leafanvil-ADR-004`) `CBIT T6b` tripwires never trigger false bit-rot quarantines.
   - **Case 2B (`palette[0]` is transmissive `cave_air` / `water` / `lava` — `< 8%` hollow cavern sections):** Omitting unexposed ore indices directly would erroneously turn buried cave-ceiling ores into `cave_air` / `lava` pockets. In Case 2B, culled voxel indices removed from `xraySensitive` containers are **unioned into the container of `lithicHostRuntimeStateId[i]`** (`palette[q]`, e.g. `deepslate`), leaving `octreeOccupancyMask64` invariant and healing ore gaps inside existing rock runs without creating air pockets.
3. **Immutable Cold `wireSanitizedBaseFrame`, `100%` `ODCT` (`LcaOrderlessDictTable`) Deduplication & `0`-Tick Block-Break Reveal:**
   - `wireSanitizedBaseFrame` is immutable once synthesized for a cold `RawChunkFrameSnapshot` (rebuilt only on off-thread CoW compaction flush in `installFlushedColdChunkFrame`, never mutated in place during live ticks) and depends solely on chunk state—preserving `100%` content-hash slot hits in `LcaOrderlessDictTable` across all players.
   - Live `TrackedChunkColumn#mutateVoxel` and `#recordDirtyVoxel` transitions write `4 B` `packDirtyOverlayEntry(pos12, authoritativeRuntimeId)` entries into `SparseDirtyCellMap`, publish the updated `FaceBitmap256` and `sectionHash64[i]` via release semantics, and inspect the `6` face neighbors (intra-chunk and across canonical neighbor `FaceBitmap256` borders); any neighbor whose authoritative state or `BlockEntity` was suppressed in `wireSanitizedBaseFrame` is appended to the same tick's outbound `EntryPatchDelta`, revealing newly exposed ores on the exact tick the covering block breaks.

### 3. Layer 3 — `LCA_NET_V1` (`PROTOCOL_VERSION = 1`) Partial-Slab Wire Framing, XOR-Composable `SEC-DED-64` & Ecosystem Boundaries

Because `:resource:LeafAnvil:leafanvil-net` is pre-release, all framing extensions land directly in `PROTOCOL_VERSION = 1` across `:anvil-api` (`LcaRemoteStreamCodec`) and `:resource:LeafAnvil:leafanvil-common` (`LcaWireStreamProtocol`, `LcaClientWireReceiver`, `LcaMultiTargetConsolidator`):

1. **Exact Cross-Language Binary Wire Grammar (`Java` / `Rust lca-format` / `TypeScript LeafMap`):**
   - `CAP_SUBTERRANEAN_OCCLUSION_STREAM = 1 << 6` added to `LcaWireStreamProtocol.ALL_CAPABILITIES`.
   - **`SectionBitMask` Encoding (`4 B` for `24`-Section Overworld, `1..254` Sections Safe):** Encoded deterministically as `uint8 maskByteLen` (`ceil(sectionCount / 8)`, `1..32`) followed by little-endian `byte[maskByteLen]` where section $i$ corresponds to `(maskBytes[i >>> 3] >>> (i & 7)) & 1` (`3 B` total for `16`-section Nether, `4 B` total for `24`-section Overworld, scaling to `254`-section custom datapack worlds with zero endianness ambiguity or 32-bit shift overflow).
   - In the existing `16-bit` entry descriptor word (`packEntryHeaderWord16(entryType, flags4, localChunkIndex10)`), the two remaining `flags4` bits (`bits 10` and `13`) are assigned without colliding with `HEADER_FLAG_ORDERLESS_DICT_SECTIONS` (`1 << 11`) or `HEADER_FLAG_HAS_HEIGHTMAPS` (`1 << 12`):
     - `HEADER_FLAG_PARTIAL_COMPLETION = 1 << 10`: Present on `EntryFullChunk` and progressive-upgrade `EntryPatchDelta`; appends `SectionBitMask completedSections` denoting which vertical sections in the client's merged column are now authoritative vs. placeholder `lithicHost`.
     - `HEADER_FLAG_HAS_SECTION_BLOCK_ENTITIES = 1 << 13`: Appends `SectionBitMask beSections` followed, for each set section bit in ascending index order, by `VarInt beCount` entries of `(uint16 pos12, VarInt beTypeRegistryId, VarInt nbtByteLen, byte[nbtByteLen] updateTagNbt)`, so chunks with `0` visible `BlockEntity` instances pay **`0` extra bytes**.
2. **Disambiguated `EntryPatchDelta` Slice Modes & Occluded Block-Delta Suppression (Closing Redstone/Piston Radar ESP):**
   - `EntryPatchDelta` replaces its fixed `int32 dirtySectionBitmask32` with `SectionBitMask patchedSections` when `sectionCount > 32` (retaining `4-byte` `int32` wire parity when `sectionCount <= 32` and `HEADER_FLAG_PARTIAL_COMPLETION` is unset) and distinguishes two unambiguous per-section update modes in `SectionWireSlice`:
     - **Mode I — Incremental Overlay Cell Merge (`!isUniform(directoryWord16) && baseFrameSlice.length == 0 && dirtyOverlayEntries.length > 0`):** Valid *only* for sections already marked `1` in the client's `completedSections`; merges `4-byte` `dirtyOverlayEntries` onto the client's existing `LcaSectionBacking` in place (used by `PaperLcaNettyChannelHandler#flushCoalescedPatchDeltas`).
     - **Mode R — Authoritative Section Replacement (`isUniform(directoryWord16) || baseFrameSlice.length > 0`):** Replaces the entire `(baseFrame, dirtyOverlay)` backing for newly completed (`0 -> 1`) or compacted sections and promotes those bits in `completedSections`.
   - **Occluded Activity-Radar Suppression (`WORLD_BLOCK_DELTA` Savings):** When a block mutates inside section $i$ (redstone clocks, pistons, hoppers, furnaces, or underground mining) for a watching player whose `sentSectionMask` currently has bit $i == 0$:
     - If the mutation does **not** connect section $i$ into the player's reachable `completedSections`, outbound `SingleBlockUpdate` / `MultiBlockSectionUpdate` / Mode I `EntryPatchDelta` packets for section $i$ are **suppressed** for that player (`0` wire bytes, eliminating active-base Redstone/Piston Radar ESP and avoiding client `SEC-DED-64` desyncs).
     - If the mutation breaches an octant barrier and promotes section $i$ (`0 -> 1`), the server emits a Mode R replacement slice (`wireSanitizedBaseFrame` + merged `dirtyOverlay` + newly exposed `BlockEntity` tags).
3. **Section-Scoped & Exposure-Gated `BlockEntity` Withholding (Closing ChestESP / SpawnerESP):**
   - `BlockEntity` entries located inside masked sections (`bit i == 0` in `completedSections`) or belonging to unexposed Layer 2 `xraySensitive` blocks (buried chests, barrels, shulker boxes, mob/trial spawners, vaults) are **strictly omitted** from `EntryFullChunk`.
   - When an `EntryPatchDelta` upgrades section $i$ (`0 -> 1` Mode R) or reveals a newly exposed buried container (`Mode I`), `HEADER_FLAG_HAS_SECTION_BLOCK_ENTITIES` delivers that section's `BlockEntity` update tags atomically in the same `ClientOrderedBatchCommitQueue` batch.
4. **XOR-Composable Per-Section `SEC-DED-64` Syndromes & Partial-Slab `ClientChunkSlabCache` (`99.9%` Revisit Preserved):**
   - Storing a masked chunk under the server's 24-section `baselineSecDed64` would either fail client `verifySecDedSyndrome64` or poison later underground revisits with solid placeholder rock.
   - Instead, each `TrackedChunkColumn` caches per-section 64-bit digests `sectionHash64[i]` (`long[sectionCount]` over the wire-sanitized slice + overlay of section $i$) and per-section `sectionUpdateTick[i]`. For any `SectionBitMask` $M$, both server and client evaluate the mask-scoped `SEC-DED-64` syndrome in $O(\text{popcnt}(M))$ cycles (`< 10 ns` for `24` sections, zero byte-array allocation):
     $$S(T_M, M) = \operatorname{secdedMix64}(T_M \oplus \operatorname{hashMask64}(M)) \oplus \bigoplus_{i \in M} \operatorname{rotl64}(\mathtt{sectionHash64}[i],\, i \bmod 64)$$
     where $T_M = \max_{i \in M} \mathtt{sectionUpdateTick}[i]$.
   - `LcaWireStreamProtocol.CachedChunkSlab` and `ChunkDigestEntry` (in `ServerboundLcaChunkDigestPayload` / `LcaRemoteStreamCodec.ChunkDigestPayload`) carry `SectionBitMask completedSections`:
     - **Surface Revisit Hit (`2 B` `EntryCleanAck`):** If a player revisits a chunk on the surface (`requiredMask & ~clientMask == 0`) and no section in `clientMask` mutated since `clientDigest.lastUpdateTick()`—even if another player mined deep underground outside `clientMask`—the server verifies $S(T_{\text{clientMask}}, \text{clientMask})$ and replies with a **`2-byte` `EntryCleanAck`**, while `ClientChunkSlabCache` stores ~`2x` more surface-traversed chunks inside its `32 MiB` budget.
     - **Progressive Underground Completion (`EntryPatchDelta`, Lifting `MAX_PATCH_DELTA_SECTIONS = 4`):** When `missingMask = requiredMask & ~clientMask` is non-empty (or `1..sectionCount` sections changed), `resolveServerRevisitEntry` emits a Mode R `EntryPatchDelta` carrying `patchedSections = missingMask | (mutatedMask & clientMask)` (`1..sectionCount` sections supported whenever estimated patch bytes `<` full-chunk bytes), upgrading `ClientChunkSlabCache`'s `completedSections` to `clientMask | missingMask` without resending the `10–14` surface/sky sections already cached on the client.
5. **3D Vertical-Stratum `MTCF` Bucketing (`LcaMultiTargetConsolidator`):**
   - `LcaMultiTargetConsolidator` partitions spatial broadcast groups by **`(cellX, cellYStratum, cellZ)`** (`SURFACE` vs. `SUBTERRANEAN` Y-bands) and unions `completedSections` across recipients within the same 3D cell—preventing a single subterranean miner at `Y = -48` beneath spawn from pulling cave sections into the shared `MTCF` multicast stream of `100+` surface players at `Y = 68`.
6. **RYOW Client `ClientChunkCache` Shell Installation, Subterranean Entity Culling & Ecosystem Isolation (`LeafShard`, `LeafMap`, `Sodium`, `Bobby`, `Distant Horizons`):**
   - **Strict RYOW `ClientOrderedBatchCommitQueue` Lifecycle (Eliminating `!hasChunk()` Drop Hazards):** When vanilla `ClientboundLevelChunkWithLightPacket` is suppressed on negotiated sessions, `FabricLeafAnvilClientMod#commitHydratedChunkOnClientMainThread` and `NeoForgeLeafAnvilClientMod#commitHydratedChunkOnClientMainThread` **must not** short-circuit on `!level.getChunkSource().hasChunk(chunkX, chunkZ)`. Instead, all `EntryFullChunk`, `EntryCleanAck`, and progressive `EntryPatchDelta` commits execute in a single main-thread FIFO (`ClientOrderedBatchCommitQueue`) fenced by a per-column `clientUnloadEpoch` (incremented on `ClientboundForgetLevelChunkPacket` so in-flight background `LCD2` decodes never resurrect unloaded chunks):
     1. `EntryFullChunk` / `EntryCleanAck` allocates-or-replaces the `LevelChunk` slot inside `ClientChunkCache` *before* section hydration, binds the `completedSections` and `lithicHostRuntimeStateId` metadata, populates `LevelLightEngine` `DataLayer` nibbles (`skyLight2 = 0` for masked rock sections), applies visible per-section `BlockEntity` update tags *during* shell construction, and fires loader chunk-load callbacks.
     2. Subsequent `EntryPatchDelta` entries dequeued in RYOW order find the `LevelChunk` shell guaranteed resident, swap upgraded `LcaSectionBacking` slices, queue newly revealed `LevelLightEngine` `DataLayer`s, purge stale section `BlockEntity` entries before attaching newly completed `BlockEntity` update tags, and invoke `ClientLevel#setSectionDirtyWithNeighbors` across the `3x3x3` section neighborhood so `Sodium` / `Embeddium` smooth-lighting seams converge cleanly.
   - **Subterranean Entity & `MinecartChest` Tracker Coupling:** Non-player entities (`MinecartChest` in sealed mineshafts, `ArmorStand` / `ItemFrame` in buried bases, subterranean mobs) located inside sections omitted from a player's `sentSectionMask` (`plus` a `1`-section boundary buffer) defer entity-tracker pairing (`ClientboundAddEntityPacket` and `LcaMultiTargetConsolidator` `ENTITY_KINEMATICS` rows) until `EntryPatchDelta` completes their containing section—saving entity bandwidth, preventing `MinecartChest`/Mob ESP leaks, and avoiding client-side suffocation jitter inside placeholder rock.
   - **`Bobby` & `Distant Horizons` Parity:** Because `surfaceEnvelopeMask` and `CAP_CLIENT_HEIGHTMAPS` remain `100%` unmasked on initial load, surface LOD columns and distant terrain caches are visually identical while using less client disk/SQLite space.
   - **`LeafShard` Inter-Server Isolation (`LCAS` `0x4C434153`):** `LcaInMemoryChunkCoordinator#exportInterServerDeltaEntry` and `#exportChunkForInterServerTransfer` **never** apply Layer 1 occlusion or Layer 2 ore culling; peer shards always replicate canonical, unmasked columns for lossless region-lease migration (`leafshard-ADR-001`..`004`).
   - **`LeafMap` Web-Stream Anti-Xray (`leafmap-ADR-004`):** Remote `LeafMap` feeds (`POST /api/stream/lca`) support `map-stream-visibility: SURFACE_ENVELOPE | FULL_WORLD` (`default = SURFACE_ENVELOPE` on survival worlds), exporting `completedSections = surfaceEnvelopeMask` via `LcaRemoteStreamCodec` so public browser `$Y$-cutaway` views cannot be abused to locate buried player stashes or dungeons.

### 4. Layer 4 — Dual-Mode Adoption (`require-lca-client`), Zero-Leak Handshake Gate & Paper Integration Tiers

1. **Pre-Handshake `AWAITING_HANDSHAKE` Spawn-Chunk Gate (Zero First-RTT Leak):**
   - Attaches per connection at `CONFIGURATION -> PLAY` entry across Paper/Folia (`PaperLeafAnvilPlugin` / `PaperLcaNettyChannelHandler`), Fabric (`MixinPlayerChunkSender`), and NeoForge (`MixinPlayerChunkSender`) *before* `PlayerChunkSender` flushes tick `0` chunks:
   - **`network.require-lca-client: false` (Default Mixed-Server Adoption Mode):** Buffers/defers initial chunk batch dispatch for `min(1 RTT, handshakeHoldTicks = 20)`. If `leafanvil:chunk_stream_handshake` succeeds, even the initial spawn chunks stream as occluded `LCA_NET_V1` frames (`0` wasted `Zlib` spawn packets); if an unmodded client begins `PLAY` movement/ack ticks without handshaking or `handshakeHoldTicks` expires, the gate transitions cleanly to `NegotiatedSession.VANILLA_FALLBACK`.
   - **`network.require-lca-client: true` (Opt-In Mandatory Mode for Modpacks & Competitive Shards):** **Zero vanilla chunk packets ever traverse the socket (`100%` fail-closed).** Clients that fail to complete `leafanvil:chunk_stream_handshake` (or fail `registryParityHash64` / `CAP_SUBTERRANEAN_OCCLUSION_STREAM`) within `handshakeTimeoutTicks` (`default = 100` ticks) are disconnected prior to receiving a single chunk byte using a configurable `REQ-RTP-S-007` kick template (`messages.yml`, e.g. Modrinth / modpack URL). On Velocity multi-server networks, lobbies can keep `require-lca-client: false` while survival/factions shards enforce `require-lca-client: true`.
2. **Paper / Folia Zero-Flag vs. `-javaagent` Tiering (`leafanvil-ADR-005` Synergy):**
   - **Zero Moonrise Storage Contention:** All Layer 1 octant masking and Layer 2 `wireSanitizedBaseFrame` filtering operate strictly on `TrackedChunkColumn`'s cold `.lca` `RawChunkFrameSnapshot` + `SparseDirtyCellMap` off the region tick thread (`REQ-RTP-S-005`, `F-001`), never mutating Moonrise's `BlockCountingBitStorage` `long[]`.
   - **Closing Non-Player Mutation Gaps in Zero-Flag Paper (`!LeafAnvilPremain.isAgentActive()`):** In `PaperLcaNettyChannelHandler#write`, outbound `SingleBlockUpdateDescriptor` and `MultiBlockSectionUpdateDescriptor` packets intercepted on watched chunks idempotently mirror `(pos12, runtimeStateId)` into `TrackedChunkColumn#recordDirtyVoxel` so TNT explosions, pistons, fluid flow, and `WorldEdit` operations update `transmissiveOctantMask64` and trigger `0`-tick neighbor ore reveals without requiring `-javaagent`. For chunks mutated while `0` players watched, `PaperLeafAnvilPlugin` refreshes stale `TrackedChunkColumn` section snapshots on `0 -> 1` watcher transitions.
   - **Eliminating Paper's Anti-Xray & Serialization Tax (`require-lca-client: true`):** On `LeafAnvil-Net`-required Paper/Folia servers, operators set `anti-xray.enabled: false` in `paper-world.yml` (`0` Paper region-thread anti-xray CPU and `0` `engine-mode: 2` packet bloat). When `-javaagent:plugins/LeafAnvil.jar` (`LeafAnvilSectionClassTransformer`) is also active with `require-lca-client: true`, `ClientboundLevelChunkPacketData` section-buffer serialization short-circuits to a **`0-byte` hollow carrier** (preserving `(chunkX, chunkZ)` and NMS `PlayerChunkSender` RTT batch-credit pacing while `PaperLcaNettyChannelHandler` emits the occluded `LCA_NET_V1` batch directly).

### 5. Mandatory TDD & Differential Parity Proof Contract (`P1..P5`)

Per [`docs/dev/VERIFICATION_GATES.md`](../../../../docs/dev/VERIFICATION_GATES.md) (Gates 1, 2, 4, 8, and 10) and `ADR-094`, implementation PRs shall author and pass five deterministic proof suites before enabling Layer 1 or Layer 2 by default:

1. **Proof Tier P1 — Compressed-Domain Optical Octant & Sightline Soundness (`LcaOctantOcclusionEngineTest`):**
   - Verifies bit-exact parity between compressed-domain `transmissiveOctantMask64` extraction and full `4,096`-voxel `AnvilChunkView` reference scans across `r.0.0.mca`, `overworld_mountain_r.0.0.mca`, Nether (`SkyLight == 0`, `hasCeiling`), End, and tall `64`-section (`sectionCount > 32`) custom datapack fixtures.
   - Asserts **zero false-positive occlusion (`falseOccludedSightlineCount == 0`)** against exhaustive 3D Amanatides-Woo voxel raycasts (including `F5` `4.0`-block third-person offsets and underground `glass` / `iron_bars` windows) across surface ravines, `1x1` vertical shafts, `120`-block cheese caverns, and `16`-chunk Nether lava seas, while proving `100%` masking of disconnected sealed caves and `0L`-transmissive solid sections.
   - Verifies `lithicHostRuntimeStateId` prevents `palette[0] == cave_air / lava` hollow-cavern leaks and verifies `> 8,191` runtime-ID (`deepslate[axis=y]`) round-trip parity without 13-bit truncation.
2. **Proof Tier P2 — Zero-Bloat Exposure Sanitizer, `CBIT T6b` Parity & Seam/Break Reveal (`LcaWireSanitizedFrameTest`):**
   - Verifies Case 2A (`palette[0]` opaque rock: `wireSanitizedBaseFrame.length <= rawBaseFrame.length`, `0` unexposed `xraySensitive` voxels present in decoded section, and `CBIT T6b` `decodedOctreeMask64 == sanitizedOctreeMask64` passes when octants empty out) and Case 2B (`palette[0]` transmissive `cave_air`: culled ceiling ores decode as `lithicHost` rock, never `cave_air`).
   - Verifies `0`-tick `4-byte` `dirtyOverlay` + `BlockEntity` reveal when an adjacent opaque block breaks (both intra-chunk and across `x = 0/15`, `z = 0/15`, `y = 0/15` boundaries), occluded block-delta suppression (`0` wire bytes emitted to surface players for non-breaching subterranean redstone/piston edits), and concurrent multi-threaded publish-before-probe chunk-seam reveal (`FaceBitmap256` CoW snapshots, `0` AB-BA locks, `0` synchronous chunk loads per `REQ-RTP-S-005`).
3. **Proof Tier P3 — Partial-Slab `SEC-DED-64` Revisit & Progressive Upgrade Protocol (`LcaPartialSlabRevisitProtocolTest`):**
   - Verifies `HEADER_FLAG_PARTIAL_COMPLETION` (`1 << 10`), `HEADER_FLAG_HAS_SECTION_BLOCK_ENTITIES` (`1 << 13`), `2-byte` `EntryCleanAck` hits on surface revisits even after unreceived subterranean sections mutate on the server, `> 4`-section (`1..24`) Mode R progressive `EntryPatchDelta` completion without resending surface sections, `1`-bit `SEC-DED-64` corruption detection/eviction, and `100%` `ODCT` (`LcaOrderlessDictTable`) slot reuse across multiple players with distinct `completedSections` masks.
4. **Proof Tier P4 — Fail-Closed Handshake, Teleport Halo & Carrier Parity (`PaperOcclusionZeroFlagAndAgentParityTest`):**
   - Verifies `0` vanilla chunk bytes emitted prior to handshake in `require-lca-client: true` mode (with configurable `REQ-RTP-S-007` kick on timeout/mismatch), zero-leak first-RTT `LCA_NET_V1` spawn streaming in `require-lca-client: false` mode, pre-teleport destination `3x3x3` halo arrival before `ClientboundPlayerPositionPacket` (`REQ-RTP-S-001`), and zero-flag Paper non-player explosion/piston delta mirroring (`!LeafAnvilPremain.isAgentActive()`).
5. **Proof Tier P5 — Differential Bandwidth, Server CPU & Client Cache Benchmark (`Gate 10`):**
   - Reports same-seed, same-machine differential measurements (`24-section LCA_NET_V1 baseline` vs. `Layer 1 + Layer 2 progressive occlusion`) across surface `LeafRTP` hops, Elytra flight, and active caving: initial wire bytes/chunk, revisit wire bytes/chunk, client `32 MiB` `ClientChunkSlabCache` resident chunk capacity, server encoding CPU (`us/chunk`), and sealed-cave/buried-ore/buried-`BlockEntity` wire suppression rate (`expected = 100.0%` for unexposed targets).

## Alternatives Considered

| Alternative | Why Rejected |
|---|---|
| Rely on Paper's built-in `ChunkPacketBlockControllerAntiXray` (`engine-mode: 1` or `2`) | Paper-only (absent on Fabric/NeoForge), currently bypassed by `PaperLcaNettyChannelHandler#write` releasing `ClientboundLevelChunkWithLightPacket`, executes on region tick threads, leaves hollow caves/structures/stashes exposed to CaveFinder/ChestESP, and bloats packets `2x–3x` with fake-ore entropy that breaks `ODCT` deduplication. |
| Full `98,304`-voxel (`24 x 4,096`) per-player 3D BFS on NMS `PalettedContainer`s | `64x` larger graph state space than `.lca` $4 \times 4 \times 4$ `transmissiveOctantMask64` bitwise shifts, couples Paper to Moonrise `BlockCountingBitStorage`, and wastes CPU unpacking solid underground sections that `.lca` skips in `< 50 ns` via section directory and micro-palette headers. |
| Isotropic short-radius BFS (`d_3D <= 48` blocks) gated solely on `SkyLight == 0` | Fails catastrophically in `minecraft:the_nether`, `minecraft:the_end`, and deep oceans below `Y = 47` (where water attenuates `SkyLight` to `0`), and clips straight visual sightlines inside giant Overworld cheese caverns, `1x1` vertical drop shafts, and underground glass windows with fake-rock walls. |
| Masking unreachable sections naively to raw `SectionWireSlice.uniform(i, palette[0], 0)` | Fails in two cases: (1) hollow cavern/aquifer sections where `palette[0]` is `cave_air`/`water`/`lava` (leaking cavern outlines and adding `Sodium` cavity triangles), and (2) `runtimeStateId > 8,191` (`deepslate[axis=y]`), which truncates under `& 0x1FFF` without the `4–5 B` Mode-`00` uniform fallback. |
| Bumping wire `PROTOCOL_VERSION` to `2` or maintaining dual legacy/partial decoders | Unnecessary migration complexity because `:resource:LeafAnvil:leafanvil-net` is pre-release and `packEntryHeaderWord16` already has `flags4` bits `10` and `13` free inside `PROTOCOL_VERSION = 1`. |

## Consequences

- **Positive:**
  - Cuts initial `LCA_NET_V1` Overworld surface-travel and `LeafRTP` chunk payloads by an additional **`~40%–65%`** (`~80%–88%+` total reduction vs. vanilla `Zlib`), fitting `7–12` surface chunks inside a single `10 KiB` vanilla congestion credit, nearly doubling `32 MiB` `ClientChunkSlabCache` chunk capacity, and eliminating unseen subterranean `Sodium` / `Embeddium` mesh triangles on the client.
  - Provides cross-platform (`Paper`, `Folia`, `Fabric`, `NeoForge`) cave, structure, buried-stash (`BlockEntity`), and buried-ore/Ancient-Debris culling at **negative wire byte cost** (`0` fake-ore entropy bloat) while preserving `100%` `ODCT` slot sharing and `MTCF` multicast scalability.
  - Lets `require-lca-client: true` Paper/Folia servers disable `paper-world.yml` `anti-xray` completely (and drop region-thread chunk packet construction to $O(1)$ with `-javaagent` hollow carriers) while keeping `require-lca-client: false` zero-friction for open public servers.
- **Negative / Trade-offs:**
  - Adds `384 B` of cached metadata per `24`-section `TrackedChunkColumn` (`long[24]` `transmissiveOctantMask64` + `long[24]` `sectionHash64`, plus compact `16x16` face bitmaps when Layer 2 is active) and a bounded `SectionBitMask` (`4 B/chunk` for `<= 32` sections) map per negotiated player (`~4 KiB` per player at `view-distance = 16`).
  - On zero-flag mixed Paper servers (`require-lca-client: false` without `-javaagent`), Paper's native `anti-xray` must remain enabled in `paper-world.yml` if operators also wish to protect unmodded `VANILLA_FALLBACK` clients.

## Invariants

- **Sightline, Optical Window & RYOW Shell Soundness (`REQ-ANVIL-OCC-001`, `REQ-RTP-S-001`):** `LcaOctantOcclusionEngine` shall classify octant transmittance by optical non-occlusion (`!isFullOpaqueCube`), shall never mask any section within a player's velocity/`F5`-swept `3x3x3`-section halo, any `surfaceEnvelopeMask` section in skylit surface dimensions, or any section reachable along an orthant-monotone transmissive sightline within `viewDistance`, shall support `sectionCount > 32` without 32-bit shift overflow, shall flush pre-teleport destination halos prior to `ClientboundPlayerPositionPacket`, and shall allocate client `LevelChunk` shells in RYOW FIFO order inside `ClientChunkCache` fenced by `clientUnloadEpoch`.
- **Opaque Lithic Mask, `CBIT T6b` Parity & `19`-Bit Runtime-ID Fidelity (`REQ-ANVIL-OCC-002`):** Masked sections and Case 2B culled voxels shall resolve strictly to an opaque terrain `lithicHostRuntimeStateId` (never transmissive `air`/`cave_air`/`water`/`lava`), Case 2A `wireSanitizedBaseFrame` slices shall re-index rank-compacted cursors against `sanitizedOctreeMask64` so `CBIT T6b` holds identically on decode, and `SectionWireSlice#maskedUniform` shall use the `4–5 B` Mode-`00` `VarInt` uniform `SectionFrame` whenever `lithicHostRuntimeStateId > 0x1FFF` (`8,191`).
- **Withheld `BlockEntity`, Occluded Delta Suppression & `0`-Tick Neighbor Reveal Parity (`REQ-ANVIL-OCC-003`, `REQ-RTP-S-004`):** `BlockEntity` payloads, non-player entity spawns, and non-breaching Mode I voxel deltas in masked sections or unexposed `xraySensitive` voxels shall never be transmitted prior to section completion or voxel exposure, and breaking/exploding/moving an adjacent occluding block shall reveal newly exposed neighbor block states and `BlockEntity` update tags within the same tick's `EntryPatchDelta`.
- **Mask-Scoped `SEC-DED-64` Revisit Integrity (`REQ-ANVIL-OCC-004`, `REQ-RTP-S-004`):** `ClientChunkSlabCache` and `ServerboundLcaChunkDigestPayload` (`ChunkDigestEntry`) shall bind every cached slab's `SEC-DED-64` syndrome to its exact `SectionBitMask` (`completedSections`) $M$; any syndrome mismatch shall evict the client slab and fall back cleanly to an `EntryFullChunk` refill.
- **Zero Main/Region-Thread Blocking I/O, Lock-Free Seam Reads & Deterministic Session Cleanup (`REQ-ANVIL-OCC-005`, `REQ-RTP-S-002`, `REQ-RTP-S-005`, `F-001`):** Neither `transmissiveOctantMask64` extraction, `wireSanitizedBaseFrame` filtering, nor horizontal seam probes shall perform synchronous chunk loads, blocking disk I/O, or cross-column lock acquisition (`FaceBitmap256` snapshots shall be immutable CoW with publish-before-probe `activeChunks` registration); per-player `sentSectionMask` entries and `AWAITING_HANDSHAKE` buffers shall be deterministically released on chunk unload, world change, and disconnect.
- **Fail-Closed Mandatory Handshake & Configurable Kick Feedback (`REQ-ANVIL-OCC-006`, `REQ-RTP-S-007`):** When `network.require-lca-client: true`, zero vanilla `ClientboundLevelChunkWithLightPacket` bytes shall be written prior to verified `leafanvil:chunk_stream_handshake` negotiation, and unmodded or mismatched clients shall be disconnected using configurable message templates (`messages.yml`).
- **Authoritative Inter-Server `LCAS` vs. Surface-Scoped `LeafMap` Isolation (`REQ-ANVIL-OCC-007`, `ADR-115`):** `LeafShard` inter-server `LCAS` (`0x4C434153`) transfers (`LcaInMemoryChunkCoordinator#exportInterServerDeltaEntry`) shall remain strictly unmasked and unsanitized (`completedSections = allSections`), while `:resource:LeafMap` survival stream ingestion (`LcaRemoteStreamCodec`) shall consume platform-neutral `:anvil-api` framing with zero horizontal dependency on `:resource:LeafAnvil`.
